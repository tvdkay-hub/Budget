Set up the phone summary

Choose a family passphrase. It locks the summary so only you two can read it, and you'll type it once on each phone. Four or more random words work well, like "kettle orange harbour seven". Capitals and extra spaces don't matter.

Which account is this from?

The app keeps each account's transactions apart. Money you move between your own accounts is left out of the budget, so it doesn't count as money in and money out.
